💡 In simple terms

Sharing 17 sweets among 5 friends gives each friend 3, with 2 left over. Python has one operator for the whole part and one for the left over.

A single slash is true division and keeps the fraction. A double slash is floor division and keeps only the whole part.

Type it yourself

17 // 5

The percent sign is the remainder operator: what is left after the whole parts are taken out. divmod gives both at once.

Predict first: floor division rounds down, not towards zero. What should -7 // 2 be?

Type it yourself 
17 % 5
divmod(17, 5)
-7 // 2
